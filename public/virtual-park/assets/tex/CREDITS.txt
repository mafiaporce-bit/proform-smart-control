Park textures. All source files are from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), licence CC0 (public domain).
They were resized, colour-graded and combined for phones with tools/mktex.py.

sky.jpg, env.jpg   from the HDRI "Park Bench" (sky kept; the winter trees and ground were removed)
grass.jpg          a patch of the lawn in the same "Park Bench" panorama, re-projected and tinted green
leaves.webp        leaf clusters assembled from the single-leaf photos of the tree model textures
fir.webp           a fir bough assembled from the fir twig photos
bark.jpg(+_n)      tree bark          firbark.jpg  fir bark
asphalt.jpg(+_n)   a gravel path scan, de-saturated and darkened to read as tarmac
kerb.jpg           flagstone path     mulch.jpg    forest floor
rock.jpg(+_n)      rock face

Made by code, not photos: bloom.webp (flower heads), water_n.jpg (water ripples).
